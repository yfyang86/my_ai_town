What is taken from it in this repository
- The six ability scores and the ability modifier rule.
- The proficiency bonus by level.
- The list of eighteen skills and the ability each is based on.
- The ability check procedure: d20 + modifiers against a Difficulty Class.
- The class names fighter, rogue, cleric and wizard.

Where it is used
- crates/protocol/src/game_mode/ruleset.rs
- crates/kernel/src/game_mode/srd5.rs
- frontend/town-shared/src/prefabs/lighthouse.ts (character sheets and checks for the optional rules switch)

Only material in SRD 5.1 may be added. Content that is not in the SRD (settings, named characters, and creatures
or spells outside it) must not appear. Each addition extends the list above.
