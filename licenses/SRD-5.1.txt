What is taken from it in this repository
- The six ability scores and the ability modifier rule.
- The proficiency bonus by level.
- The list of eighteen skills and the ability each is based on.
- The ability check procedure: d20 + modifiers against a Difficulty Class.
- The class names fighter, rogue, cleric and wizard, and for each: hit die, saving-throw proficiencies,
  the skill list and number of choices, and the rogue's Expertise.
- Hit points at 1st level and at higher levels (fixed average).
- Saving throws; advantage and disadvantage; contests; passive checks; the Help action;
  the two skill proficiencies granted by a background.
- Combat: initiative, the actions Attack, Dash, Disengage, Dodge and Help, opportunity attacks,
  attack rolls against Armor Class, critical hits, dropping to 0 hit points and death saving throws.
- Ten creatures (bandit, guard, thug, cultist, goblin, skeleton, zombie, wolf, giant rat, mastiff):
  Armor Class, hit points, Dexterity and one attack each.
- Nine weapons and four armours with their damage dice and Armor Class, and the shield.
- Spellcasting: spell slots by class level for cleric and wizard (levels 1-3), spellcasting ability,
  spell attack bonus and spell save DC, cantrips, casting with a higher-level slot, concentration.
- Fourteen spells: Fire Bolt, Ray of Frost, Sacred Flame, Magic Missile, Burning Hands, Thunderwave,
  Guiding Bolt, Inflict Wounds, Cure Wounds, Healing Word, Bless, Scorching Ray, Hold Person,
  Prayer of Healing - level, range reduced to "touch / at range / an area", damage or healing dice,
  saving throw and concentration.
- The fighter's Second Wind and the rogue's Sneak Attack.
- The long rest; experience points by creature and the experience needed for levels 2 and 3.
- The table of typical Difficulty Classes (5 to 25) and the standard set of ability scores
  (15, 14, 13, 12, 10, 8), used by the narrator's rulings and by drafted character sheets.
